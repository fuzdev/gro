feat: `test`, `lint`, `typecheck`, and `sync` take their handles from their `host`, their bodies `run_test`, `run_lint`, `run_typecheck`, and `run_sync`; a builtin's program grant, a `BuiltinProgramGrant`, is keyed by role, the name of the task arg or config field that may choose its program (`eslint_cli`, `svelte_check_cli`, `typescript_cli`, `pm_cli`) or its fixed tool's name with `_cli` (`vitest_cli`, `sveltekit_cli`, `git_cli`), with its default `name`, `name_from` (`'arg'` or `'config'`) when another name may come from the arg or config field the role names, `local_first` to look in the project's `node_modules/.bin` before `PATH`, and `pass_env`, `omit_env`, and `env` for its environment; the `stdout` and `stderr` grants give the `host` the root's streams, and every task's `TaskContext` has `implicit_rest_args`, the forwarded section it reads as its own without naming a tool: the CLI's first section after `--` for the task the CLI invoked (none when it starts with `gro`), so `gro test -- --watch` and a project task run as `gro task <path> -- x` read theirs from the context rather than the process's args, and for a task another invokes none unless the invoking task's `invoke_task` passes one in its new `implicit_rest_args` option, as a wrapper forwarding its section to `test` does

**breaking** A `BuiltinProgramGrant` requires `name`, and `host.programs.program` and `find` take `(role, name?)`, refusing a name other than the default unless the grant has `name_from`. `BuiltinHostRoot` requires `stdout` and `stderr`, `TaskContext` requires `implicit_rest_args`, so a hand-built context needs one, a `PluginContext` too, `RunTaskOptions` requires it, and `InvokeTaskOptions` and `InvokeTaskContextOptions` take it, `invoke_cli` setting it for the task it invokes. `to_implicit_forwarded_argv(task_name, cli_args, root, command?)` is removed: a task reads its section from its context, and `to_implicit_rest_args(raw_rest_args)` is how a root computes it, none when it starts with `gro`, a task dropping one starting with its own tool's name, as `test` drops `vitest`. `sveltekit_sync` and `sveltekit_sync_if_obviously_needed` take a `CliFinder` (`to_cli_finder(spawn, SVELTEKIT_PROGRAM_OPTIONS)` at a root) in place of a `Spawn`, and `install_with_cache_healing` and `install_with_cache_healing_or_throw` an `InstallPrograms` (`to_install_programs(spawn)` at a root); `to_install_program_options` is replaced by the data `PM_CLI_PROGRAM_GRANT`. `to_git_program` takes a `Spawn` with its options, now required, or a builtin's `host.programs` with none, and `GitFinder` is the latter's `find(role, name?)` with the `git_cli` role, which a `Spawn` doesn't fit. `test.task.ts`, `lint.task.ts`, `typecheck.task.ts`, and `sync.task.ts` export `task` as a `BuiltinTask`, so run them by name or call their bodies with plain objects; by path they're refused. Importing `test.task.ts` or `typecheck.task.ts` no longer reads the working directory in its own module scope, their `dir` and `cwd` defaults made when the args are parsed.
